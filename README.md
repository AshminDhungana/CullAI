# CullAI
AI-powered photo culling for Windows, Mac &amp; Linux. Supports RAW formats (CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, DNG), face &amp; eye detection, genre presets, and adjustable scoring weights. Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Ollama locally. Exports XMP sidecars for Lightroom &amp; Capture One. Your photos never leave your machine.
